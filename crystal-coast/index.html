Plan Your Beach DayCondition-aware tips
📋

Saturday's heat is no joke on the Crystal Coast, with a heat advisory in effect until evening and heat index values topping 102 degrees. The bright side is mostly sunny weather through early afternoon with 2 to 3 foot surf and water temps in the mid 80s, but plan to head out early because scattered thunderstorms roll in after 2 PM with about a 30 to 41% chance of rain. Sunday shapes up much nicer with a slight chance of thunderstorms, cooler air moving in (high of 85), and northeast winds dropping to 7 to 12 mph, so if Saturday's heat and storm risk feel like a wash, Sunday is worth the wait.

🐚 ShellingTides and beach walks
🐚 Shelling conditionsExcellent

Shell yeah! Excellent shelling conditions mean Saturday's low tide window is worth planning around. Look along the fresh wrack line and just below the last high-tide mark, especially on quieter stretches with less foot traffic. For the best odds, walk slowly within about an hour of low tide and check shell piles near inlets, jetties, and gently sloped sandbars. Saturday's cue: Low tide at 11:14 AM (-0.3 ft), prime shelling window.

Best timingWithin about an hour before or after low tide.
Where to lookWrack lines, inlet edges, shell piles, and sandbar drop-offs.
Good findsOlive shells, whelk pieces, coquinas, sand dollars, and Scotch bonnet fragments.

🐾 Paws at the BeachLocal ordinances · verify before you go
Are dogs allowed at the beach?
Yes, but rules vary by town. Check each town before you go.
Beach-by-beach dog rules
✅Emerald Isle
Allowed on the beach strand year-round, no seasonal restriction
Leash required at all times (16-ft max). Waste cleanup required.
⚠️Atlantic Beach
Leashed 10 AM-6 PM during high season (summer); more relaxed off-season under voice control
Leash max 12 ft. Dogs may swim off-leash briefly in the ocean with their owner, then must be re-leashed before returning to the beach.
✅Pine Knoll Shores
Allowed on the beach year-round
Leash required at all times (16-ft max). Waste cleanup required.
⚠️Cape Lookout National Seashore
Allowed throughout the seashore, including on the beach
Leash required at all times (6-ft max, stricter than the mainland towns). Keep dogs away from the wild horses and shorebirds. Ferry access only, check your ferry operator's pet policy before booking.
Rules are subject to change. Always verify current ordinances with the town or the National Park Service before visiting.
